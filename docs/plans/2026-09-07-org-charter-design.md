## 1. Overview
The Founder established the organizational structure for the standalone QS Quantification Engine consisting of:
- **Founder** (Product vision, business priorities, milestone acceptance)
- **CTO** (System architecture, quality standards, deterministic vs. AI enforcement)
- **Backend Developer** (Geometry parsing, topology reconstruction, QS calculation rules)
- **Frontend Developer** (Interactive CAD/PDF drawing canvas, visual verification overlay, exception review)
- **Tester / QA Engineer** (Deterministic test suites, tolerance verification, benchmark test corpus)

## 2. Decision Record
1. **Operating Model**: Hybrid approach. Formal organizational charter documented in repository, accompanied by an active role-tagged AI persona workflow during development sessions.
2. **Communication Pattern**: Explicit role-tagging (`[CTO]`, `[Backend Dev]`, `[Frontend Dev]`, `[QA Engineer]`) to ensure full transparency, code review rigor, and QA verification prior to Founder acceptance.
3. **Core Mandate**: AI interprets ambiguous notes/symbols; deterministic computational geometry calculates all quantities. Zero hallucinated quantities.
